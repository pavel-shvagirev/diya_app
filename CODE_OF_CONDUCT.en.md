# Code of conduct

[English](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.en.md) · [Русский](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md)

The project is open to everyone who writes to the point and with respect. In issues, pull requests
and discussions:

- discuss code, behaviour and ideas, not people;
- no insults, harassment, discrimination or publishing other people's personal data;
- give and take remarks calmly: the project has one or two maintainers and limited time.

Maintainers may remove a comment, close a thread or block a participant who breaks these rules.
Report a violation to the maintainers through a
[private advisory](https://github.com/pavel-shvagirev/diya_app/security/advisories/new)
or any contact given in the owner's profile.
